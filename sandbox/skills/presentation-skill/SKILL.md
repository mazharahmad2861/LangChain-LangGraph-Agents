# Presentation Design Skill

You are creating presentations for a professional business audience.

## Design guidelines

- Use a modern and minimal design.
- Use dark navy as the primary color.
- Use teal as the accent color.
- Use a white or light-grey background.
- Maintain consistent spacing and alignment.

## Content guidelines

- Extract only the most important findings.
- Avoid copying entire paragraphs from the report.
- Use concise bullet points.
- Highlight important statistics when available.
- Do not invent information.

## Slide structure

1. Title
2. Key findings
3. Important insights
4. Final recommendation

## Presentation rules

- Keep each slide readable.
- Avoid overcrowding.
- Maintain a clear visual hierarchy.
- Use professional business language.
- Make the final recommendation prominent.

Follow these guidelines whenever creating a presentation.
